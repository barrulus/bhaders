Play around.

Every download includes editable source, a preset and instructions. No account. No installer. Just the effect you came for.

  1. 01

    Choose your compositor

    Biri uses KDL presets. Umbriel uses TOML effect definitions. Choose a compatible version in the effect details.

  2. 02

    Keep the files together

    Extract to ~/.config/bhaders/, preserving the folders. Each ZIP includes the files its preset needs.

  3. 03

    Include, validate, reload

    Follow the effect's instructions to activate it. These need the custom shader features in Biri or bumbriel; stock niri and older Umbriel builds are not interchangeable.
